Orders

IDCustomerTypeAmountStatusActions

These orders stand in for what Command Cloud would normally hand off once a delivery is ready to bill. For COD orders, "COD check" runs a simulated pre-delivery payment verification, and "Dispatch" is blocked until that check clears, modeling a merchant confirming payment is collectible before a truck rolls. "Simulate payment" then mimics the customer actually paying, which is what drives the reconciliation, funding, and notification sections below.

Transactions

IDOrderMethodAmountStatusActions

"Simulate return" mimics money coming back after a payment already succeeded, such as an ACH return (e.g. insufficient funds) or a card chargeback (e.g. cardholder dispute), distinct from a merchant-initiated refund. It reopens the linked invoice and fires a payment.returned webhook.
